## Rules represented in the planner

- For the standard schedule, Article 126 states at least 20 working days for a five-day week and 24 working days for a six-day week; other weekly patterns use a four-week minimum. Other categories and provisions provide for longer entitlements in some circumstances. The tool therefore presents 20/24 only as common standard minimums and asks visitors to enter their own actual remaining leave balance.
- Article 126 excludes statutory holidays from annual-leave duration. Statutory holidays therefore do not consume the tool's leave-day budget. Saturday/Sunday holidays are not moved to Monday by this calculation.
- Article 128 requires leave at least once each working year and generally requires one annual-leave portion to be at least 10 working days for a five-day week or 12 for a six-day week; for fewer or variable workdays, the portion is expressed as two weeks.

## Scope limitations shown to visitors

The planner does not determine an individual's statutory entitlement or accrued balance; handle extended/additional leave categories, part-year accrual, variable shifts, summarized working-time accounting, pay, or employer-specific rules; or represent employer approval. It compares dates against the visitor-supplied schedule, budget, and constraints. Its suggestions are planning estimates, not a legal opinion or an approved leave request.
